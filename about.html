About Apex Hearo Construction

Local, licensed, and built on referrals from homeowners who trust us with their biggest asset.

Our Story

Local craftsmanship, built the right way

Apex Hearo Construction started with a simple frustration: too many Orange County homeowners were getting burned by contractors who disappeared mid-project, missed deadlines, or padded their invoices after the fact. We set out to be the opposite — a general contractor homeowners could actually trust with the biggest investment they'll ever make.

That means every project runs under our own license, with our own crew — not a rotating cast of subcontractors. From a single-day repair to a full ADU build, you get one dedicated point of contact, a written scope before we break ground, and a team that shows up when we say we will.

Our Mission

Craftsmanship you can trust, pricing you can plan around

We believe homeowners deserve clear pricing, realistic timelines, and craftsmanship that holds up long after the crew leaves. No vague estimates that balloon mid-project and no disappearing subcontractors — just honest communication and quality work, on every home we touch.
